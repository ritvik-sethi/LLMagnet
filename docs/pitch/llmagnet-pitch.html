The Idea

Written for readers. Structured for the models that cite them.

Readers increasingly ask AI assistants instead of typing into a search box. When they do, the source the model quotes is the one that gets seen.

LLMagnet is built to help a reporter prepare a story for both audiences at once — the person reading it, and the model that may cite it — without treating them as separate jobs.

How It's Built

Six tools, connected around one draft.

One draft

Shared workspace

Your article lives in one place and moves through every tool. It helps you avoid re-pasting your work each time you switch tools.

Two views

The council

Each tool shows a reader's view and a model's view of your draft. It helps you improve for AI without losing readability.

One path

A guided flow

A simple progress view walks the story from first draft to publish-ready. It helps you see where you are and what's left.
